Healthcare organizations are managing increasingly complex workflows across clinical, financial, and payer-driven processes that don’t always work seamlessly together. These areas include patient payments, utilization management review, and patient billing — where RCM innovations like agentic AI and advanced automation can quickly improve efficiency and outcomes. However, identifying the right solutions and successfully integrating them can still be difficult.
